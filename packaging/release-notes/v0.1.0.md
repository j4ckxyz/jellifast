Jellifast 0.1.0 is the first version: a native music player for Jellyfin, built on the interface of the Spotify client it was forked from.

## New

- **Sign in to a Jellyfin server** with its address, your user name and your password. Only the access token is kept, in the system credential store.
- **Your library**: every album and album artist, playlists, favourite songs as Liked Songs, recently added, recently played and most played.
- **Playback on this computer**: original files where they decode directly (MP3, FLAC, AAC, ALAC, Vorbis, WAV, AIFF), the server's MP3 conversion otherwise, with a queue, shuffle, repeat, seeking, gapless albums and volume normalisation.
- **Playlists**: create, rename, describe, change the cover, add, remove, reorder and delete.
- **Instant Mix** as song, album, artist and playlist radio, and as autoplay.
- **Lyrics** from the server, with LRCLIB as the fallback.
- **Play reporting**, so the server's play counts and history stay true.
- **Other players**: control another Jellyfin client on the same server, or hand it the queue.
